# Terms of Use Draft

**Status:** Owner review required before invited real-data testing.
**Last updated:** 2026-09-11

Cashflow Simulator provides an estimate of future cash position from the dates, amounts, recurrence rules, business-day assumptions, and bill flexibility that a user enters. It is not a bank service, accounting system, payment service, credit product, or financial adviser.

## Estimates and User Responsibility

Projections can be wrong because actual balances, posting dates, holidays, transaction amounts, and institution practices can differ. Users must verify important decisions with their financial institution and should not rely on the simulator as financial advice.

## Recommendations

Recommendations are constrained, hypothetical scheduling scenarios. Apply and Undo change the simulator scenario only. They never authorize, schedule, or execute a payment.

## Accounts and Data

Users are responsible for keeping account credentials confidential and for entering data they are permitted to use. Users may export their account data or permanently delete their account from Settings. Deletion removes application records and cannot be undone.

## Availability

The service may change, be interrupted, or be retired. The owner does not promise uninterrupted availability or a particular financial outcome.

Before publication, the owner must add the legal operator, governing law, contact information, effective date, acceptable-use terms, limitation-of-liability language, and any required consumer disclosures.
