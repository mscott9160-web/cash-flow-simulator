## What To Include In A Support Request

Report the screen or workflow, approximate time, device/browser, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords, access tokens, bank credentials, full account numbers, or unnecessary financial details.

For projection questions, include the relevant rule shape rather than private account information, such as whether the item was a fixed, window, or flexible bill and the intended date range.

## Account Recovery and Data Requests

The published support channel must explain how to request help with account access, export, deletion, or suspected unauthorized access. Account deletion is available from Settings after authentication.

## Owner Review Items

Before publication, add the support email or ticket URL, expected response window, security-reporting path, outage-status URL if one exists, and escalation owner. Do not publish a personal address without confirming that it is intended for support use.
